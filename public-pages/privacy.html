Privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026

This policy describes the Gather CRM service at gather-crm.vercel.app. For privacy questions or account-data requests, contact the service operator at rounakchhatait@gmail.com.

Information Gather stores

  • Your account: name, email address, a password hash when you use a password, and your Google account identifier when you register with or link Google. Gather does not store your Google password.
  • Your CRM content: workspace details, business-card photos, extracted or entered contacts, phone numbers and email addresses, spreadsheet references, templates, images, campaign recipients, and sending results.
  • Connections and settings: Google authorization tokens, the connected email address, and the AI provider, model, endpoint, and API key you configure.
  • Operational information: session records, temporary OAuth authorization state, unsuccessful login attempts, and registration rate-limit records. The hosting provider may process request metadata such as IP address and technical error logs to run the service.

How Google information is used

Google sign-in retrieves your verified email, account identifier, and available profile name to authenticate you. Its sign-in access token is discarded after that exchange.

Connecting Gmail separately grants permission to send email on your behalf. Gather sends the templates, recipient details, and images you select to Gmail when you confirm a campaign. It records Gmail's message identifier and sending result. It does not request permission to read your inbox.

Connecting Google Sheets separately grants spreadsheet read/write access. Gather uses it to create or link a workspace spreadsheet, import rows you choose, and synchronize that workspace's contacts. The permission can cover your spreadsheets broadly, while the application's operations target the spreadsheets connected through your workspaces.

Gather's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gather does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

AI extraction and other service providers

When AI extraction is enabled, uploading a card automatically queues its image and extraction instructions for transmission to the AI provider and endpoint selected in your account's Settings. Cards uploaded before setup wait until a model and key are saved and extraction is enabled; the open Gather app then processes the waiting cards. Model listing and configuration tests also contact that provider. Gather does not send your whole spreadsheet or inbox to an AI provider for this feature. Review the selected provider's data practices before uploading personal information.

Vercel hosts the application and processes requests. Neon PostgreSQL stores the application's encrypted records and images. Google processes sign-in requests and the Gmail/Sheets operations you authorize; interface fonts may also be loaded from Google. These providers process information needed to supply their services and may operate in locations outside your country.

Storage and access

CRM records and images are stored encrypted in the hosted database. Saved connection tokens and AI keys are also encrypted by the application. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Each signed-in account receives its own CRM data; another ordinary account cannot access your records. Authorized service operation, troubleshooting, and privacy requests may require operator access. Encryption does not mean that only you can decrypt the information.

Gather uses essential cookies for sign-in and OAuth security, and browser storage for interface preferences such as the selected workspace. The application does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Retention, disconnecting and deletion requests

Account and CRM records remain stored until removed by the operator or through available record-editing controls. Disconnecting Gmail or Sheets removes that connection's stored tokens from the active CRM record; it does not delete your stored contacts, campaigns, sent Gmail messages, or Google spreadsheets. You can also revoke permission in your Google Account connections.

To request account deletion or removal of stored CRM data, email rounakchhatait@gmail.com and identify your Gather account. The operator may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. The application does not yet offer a self-service account-deletion button. Protected backup copies may retain earlier records until those backups are retired; deletion from Gather does not remove copies already sent to recipients or saved in external services.

Policy changes

Changes to these data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Contact the operator with questions before using a feature whose data handling you do not understand.
