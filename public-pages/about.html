Gather CRM brings business cards, contacts, spreadsheets, and email outreach into one place. Keep every occasion organised in its own workspace.

Create your account →Sign in
01 / CAPTURE

From card to contact

Upload visiting-card photos. Use your configured AI provider to extract names, business details, phone numbers, and every visible email address. Review and correct the results before saving.

02 / ORGANISE

A workspace for each occasion

Keep separate contact lists and templates for events or projects. Connect one Google spreadsheet to each workspace, import contacts, and review duplicates when copying between workspaces.

03 / FOLLOW UP

Your message, sent through Gmail

Write templates, add images, and review recipients before confirming a campaign. Each selected address receives an individual email. See confirmed sends and delivery errors in Communication.

Your accounts stay separate

Every Gather account has its own workspaces, contacts, images, templates, and connections. Sign in with email and password or Google. Connect Gmail and Google Sheets separately when you want to use them.

What Google access is used for

Google sign-in uses your verified identity to create or access your Gather account. Gmail permission lets Gather send the campaigns you confirm; Gather does not request inbox-reading access. Google Sheets permission lets Gather create, read, and update spreadsheets you connect through a workspace.

Card extraction uses the AI provider you choose in Settings. Sending a saved email template does not require AI. See the privacy policy for data storage, provider sharing, and account controls.

Gather CRM is an independent application. Google login and connector availability are subject to Google's authorization and review requirements.
